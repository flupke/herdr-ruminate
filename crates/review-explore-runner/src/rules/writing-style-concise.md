### Concise writing style

Apply this rule to every text the reviewer reads: the design, each question with its Context,
choices, Door, Blast radius and notes, your replies, the conclusion, the quiz and your replies
in the round conversation.

Be extremely concise. Sacrifice grammar for the sake of concision: this style takes the place of
the full sentences that Texts the reviewer reads asks for.

The style governs wording and length only: keep every fact, citation, assessment and question
the other sections ask for, and the context that a reviewer who was away needs to follow a
text on its own (see Texts the reviewer reads). Technical names stay exactly as they are in
the code. Code, citations, diagrams and quoted text are not prose: do not rewrite them.
